ocr.siliconwat.dev: drawings

ocr.siliconwat.dev will be a free page from Silicon Wat℠ that reads Pāli and Khmer printed in Khmer script, from a photo, a scan or a PDF of the Buddhist Institute edition of the Tipiṭaka (1927–69), entirely in the reader's own browser. Every screen is drawn on a phone in English and Khmer, with the empty and result screens on a computer and in dark mode.

The icon is a placeholder: Silicon Wat's own mark is not settled.
