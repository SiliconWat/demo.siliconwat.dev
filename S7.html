What it reads

Pāli and Khmer printed in Khmer script: both pages of each opening of the Buddhist Institute edition of the Tipiṭaka (Phnom Penh, 1927–69). It tells which one a page is, and you can switch it.

How well it reads

On Pāli pages the models never saw, Most accurate got 1.5% of characters wrong, and Public domain 2.3%. Khmer pages: [ figure to come ].

It was trained on one edition's typeface and learned from one transcriber's typing, including some of their habits. The Vinaya volumes read least well, and photos from a phone have not been measured yet.

Check what it reads against the print.

Your privacy

Your images and text are read on this device and never uploaded: the page is built so that it cannot send them. Two things leave only when you press: a passage you choose to hear (to our voice service and Azure) and a passage you ask about (to the assistant you chose). Visits are counted without cookies, and the count never sees an image or a word.

The two readers

Most accurate: Apache-2.0. Public domain: CC0. The license matters only if you share a model file itself. The training data is not released.
